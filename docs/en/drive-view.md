Drive opens as cards. A folder card says **Folder**. A PDF card says **PDF**.

The button beside **Type** changes the view.

1. Click it once. The files become a **List**.
2. Click it again. They go back to **Grid**.

When you are looking at cards, the icon on the button is the list. When you are looking at a list, the icon is the grid.
