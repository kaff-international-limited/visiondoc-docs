# Workspace

**Workspace** is the lower part of the sidebar. What you see depends on your role. Company administrators see the setup items. Other people see the items their role allows.

| Item | Who it is for | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| Signing | Anyone who is asked to sign | Open documents waiting for your signature |
| Form designer | Someone who can change settings | Lay out the forms your company uses |
| Rules | Someone who can change settings | Decide how incoming files are filed and named |
| Audit | People in the workspace | Read the record of what happened |
| People & access | Someone who can change settings | Add people and set what each role can do |
| Security | You | Your own account security |
| Settings | Someone who can change settings | Company workspace settings |
| Staff | VisionDoc staff only | Staff tools. Company users do not see this |

## Language and theme

The language control is at the bottom of the sidebar. It switches the console between the languages your company is offered, including English and Traditional Chinese.

The theme control next to it switches light and dark for the console. Your choice stays on this browser.

## Your account

The round button with your initial opens **Your account**. Use it to see the account you are signed in as. **Log out** is beside it.
