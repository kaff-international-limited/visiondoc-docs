# Workspace

**Workspace** is the lower group in the sidebar. Company administrators see the setup items. Other people see what their role allows.

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Signing | Documents waiting for a signature |
| Rules | How incoming files are filed and named |
| Audit | Who read, moved, corrected, or downloaded a document |
| People & access | People in the company, and what each role can do |
| Security | Your own account |
| Settings | The company workspace |

**Audit** reads **Every read, move, correction and download, with who did it**. Filter with **All events**, **Downloads**, **Corrections**, or **Access denied**. **Export CSV** downloads that list. **Filter** narrows it further.

## Along the bottom

The sun icon switches light and dark for this browser. **EN** switches the language. The bell is notifications. The letter in the circle is your account. The door is **Log out**.

**Ask** is the round button at the bottom right of the console.
